Our story

Some love stories begin with fireworks. Ours began with a conversation that never really ended: long talks, shared laughs, and the quiet feeling that we had found our person.

Through busy seasons and small adventures, we learned that home isn't a place, it's each other. Then one evening came a small box, a trembling hand, and a very happy "yes."

Now we are ready for our next chapter, and we would love for you, the people who have loved and supported us, to be there as we say "I do" in the heart of Intramuros.

With love, Jay & Char
